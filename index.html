Why Docotia exists

I'm a dad who got curious about what AI could actually do — not as a hobby, but as a way to hand my kids something real. Not a toy. Not a gimmick. A working set of tools they could use to write their own books, build their own software, and learn in a way that fit them, not a standardized test.

Docotia Press came first, because writing was the most direct path: a finished book, on a real shelf, with our name on it. Docotia Labs came next, because some problems are better solved with software than with words — starting with a tool for people navigating limb loss, an issue close to home. Docotia Learning is next: the curriculum that ties it together, built the way we wish more curriculum was built.

None of this is a side project dressed up as a company. It's a family, doing the work, one thing at a time.

— Spencer, founder
