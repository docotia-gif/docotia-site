LimbView

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

LimbView is a mirror therapy session tracker for visual and motor imagery practice. This privacy policy explains what information LimbView collects, how it is stored, and what control you have over it.

Summary: LimbView keeps all your data on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers, cloud services, or third parties.

What LimbView collects and stores

Session data

When you use LimbView, the app records:

  • Session date and time — when you started and ended a session
  • Session duration — how long the session lasted
  • Pre-session pain score — your pain level (0–10) before the session
  • Post-session pain score — your pain level (0–10) after the session
  • Intact limb side — which side you selected (left or right)

This data is stored locally on your iPhone only. It does not leave your device unless you choose to export it.

Camera access

LimbView requests permission to access your camera to display your live reflection during sessions. The camera is used only for real-time display:

  • Live camera frames are shown during a session but never saved to disk
  • Mirror images (the processed reflection side) are displayed during the session and deleted when you end it
  • No camera data is transmitted to any server or third party
  • No video or photo files are created or retained

Where your data stays

All session records are stored in local app storage on your iPhone only. They do not sync to iCloud, email, or any cloud service. If you delete the LimbView app from your phone, all local session data is deleted.

Exporting and sharing

LimbView provides an optional feature to export session history as a PDF file. When you choose to export:

  • LimbView generates a PDF file on your device
  • The app then invokes iOS's native share sheet, which is controlled by Apple, not by Docotia
  • You choose where to send the PDF (email, Messages, Files, AirDrop, etc.)
  • LimbView does not transmit, upload, or forward the file
  • iOS handles all delivery to your chosen destination

You are in control of whether and where any export is shared.

Advertising and analytics

LimbView does not include:

  • Advertising networks or ad SDKs
  • Analytics or crash reporting
  • Third-party tracking
  • Cross-app or cross-device profiling

Third-party services

LimbView does not use third-party services that access your data. The app is self-contained and works entirely on your device.

Your choices and rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Delete sessions — you can delete individual sessions or all history from within the app
  • Revoke camera access — you can remove camera permission at any time in Settings → Apps → LimbView → Camera
  • Delete the app — deletes all local session data
  • Control exports — you decide if, when, and where to export session data

Children and age eligibility

LimbView is designed for adults ages 13 and up. If you are under 13, please do not use this app. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to Docotia, please contact us immediately at the email address below.

Medical disclaimer

LimbView is a general wellness tool for visual imagery and motor practice. It is not a medical device, diagnosis, treatment, or cure. The session tracker and pain scores are for personal tracking only.

Consult a healthcare provider before starting any new therapy, exercise program, or treatment. LimbView is a companion tool for visual practice and should not replace professional medical advice.

Changes to this policy

If we change this privacy policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use LimbView after a change means the updated policy applies.

Contact

Questions about this policy, concerns about your privacy, or requests to exercise your rights can be sent to limbview@docotia.com. We will respond to privacy inquiries within 30 days.
