LimbView

Mirror therapy session tracker for visual and motor imagery practice. Coming to the App Store.

What is mirror therapy?

Mirror therapy is a visual imagery technique where you watch a mirror reflection of your intact limb, creating the visual illusion of bilateral symmetry. This practice engages visual and motor pathways to support recovery, reduce phantom sensation, and build confidence in movement.

LimbView is a companion app that makes mirror therapy sessions easy to track and repeat. Start a session, watch your reflected image, and log how you felt before and after.

Features

  • Live mirror display — real-time reflection of your intact limb with distortion-free optics
  • Session timing — track how long you practice with a simple timer
  • Pain tracking — log your pain level (0–10) before and after each session
  • Session history — review past sessions and track trends over time
  • Export to PDF — export your session history to share with a therapist or keep as a record
  • Privacy-first — all data stays on your device, nothing is sent to our servers
  • No distractions — simple, focused UI designed for therapy, not ads or notifications

Privacy

LimbView keeps your data on your device. Your session logs, pain scores, and any exported data are yours alone.

  • All session data is stored locally on your iPhone
  • Camera frames are never saved or transmitted
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads
  • You control whether and where to export your data
