LimbView

Mirror therapy session tracker for visual and motor imagery practice. Coming to the App Store.

What is mirror imagery practice?

Mirror imagery practice is a visual technique where you observe a reflection of your intact limb, creating the visual illusion of bilateral symmetry. This practice is often recommended by therapists as part of a supervised program.

LimbView is a session logger that helps you track your mirror imagery practice. Start a session, record the duration, and log your observations before and after so you can track patterns over time.

Features

  • Session logging — record the date, time, and duration of each practice session
  • Observation tracking — log your observations (0–10 scale) before and after each session
  • Session history — review past sessions and track patterns in your observations over time
  • Export to PDF — export your session history to share with your therapist or keep as a record
  • Privacy-first — all data stays on your device, nothing is sent to our servers
  • Simple, focused UI — designed for logging practice, without distractions or ads

Privacy

LimbView keeps your data on your device. Your session logs, pain scores, and any exported data are yours alone.

  • All session data is stored locally on your iPhone
  • Camera frames are never saved or transmitted
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads
  • You control whether and where to export your data
