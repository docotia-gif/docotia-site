Amputee BMI

A body mass index calculator that accounts for limb loss. Free on the web, with an iOS app in development.

A short walkthrough of the iOS app.

Why standard BMI falls short

Standard BMI is calculated from total body weight without accounting for missing limb mass, so it reads artificially low after amputation. Amputee BMI applies validated body-segment correction to produce a weight-adjusted figure that better reflects body composition.

The calculator runs entirely in your browser. Enter your pre-amputation height, current weight, and the level of limb loss on each limb — it returns a corrected BMI on the spot, and nothing you type leaves your device.

What you can enter

  • Height before amputation — in cm/kg or ft & in/lb
  • Current weight
  • Level of limb loss, per limb — above-knee, below-knee, or foot/partial foot; above-elbow, below-elbow, or hand/partial hand — so mixed cases are handled correctly

Features

  • Body-segment correction — applies published segment-mass proportions rather than a flat adjustment
  • Per-limb input — specify the level of limb loss separately for each arm and leg
  • Two unit systems — cm & kg, or feet/inches & pounds
  • Works offline — the calculation is done on your device, with no account or sign-in
  • Privacy-first — the values you enter are never transmitted or stored
  • No ads in the app — a single-purpose tool without distractions

Availability

The free web calculator is live now at amputeebmi.com. The native iOS version is built and in preparation for release.

Privacy

The height, weight, and limb-loss values you enter are used only to perform the calculation on your device. They are never sent to us, stored on our servers, or shared with any third party. No account or sign-in is required.

The web calculator displays advertising. The iOS app does not collect personal information.
